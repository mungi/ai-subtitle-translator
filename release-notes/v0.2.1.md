## What's Changed

The biggest addition in this release is **subtitle downloads**, including **AI summaries** of a whole video.

### Highlights
- **Download subtitles** from the player menu: original or translation, as SRT (timed to the video) or TXT (text only).
- **AI summary download**: the selected LLM summarizes the full transcript in your target language and saves it as Markdown. Available when an LLM provider is selected; the summary prompt can be edited in Settings.

### Also new
- Subtitle style and subtitle box improvements: font weight, fonts installed on your PC, rounded thick outlines, and better resizing and positioning.
- Richer player menu: model names next to AI providers and a word highlight on/off switch (YouTube).
- Shows when YouTube auto-translation is in use (default).
- Temporary translation now always uses Google Translate.

### Fixes
- Changing settings no longer resets the subtitle box position and size, or choices made from the player menu.
