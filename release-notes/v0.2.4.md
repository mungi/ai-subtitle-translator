## What's Changed

This release adds **summary styles**, works on **Vimeo players embedded in other sites** without any setup, and makes **translated subtitles break more naturally**.

### Highlights
- **Summary styles**: Download subtitles → Summary in the player menu now offers Brief (slide-ready bullet points), Detailed (a structured, lecture-focused summary), and Custom (your own prompt, edited in Settings). The style you used last is remembered.
- **Faster summaries of long videos**: models with a large context (Gemini, GPT, Claude) summarize a long transcript in one request instead of many small parts. If a request is too large for the model or your account's rate limit, AST splits it and tries again.
- **Embedded Vimeo players**: Vimeo players embedded in other sites (such as conference pages), recorded or live, work without adding the site to the generic player list. Decorative background videos and very small players are skipped. On such a page, the toolbar popup shows a Vimeo switch.
- **More natural translated subtitles**: a translated sentence spread over several subtitle lines now breaks at full stops and commas, and a word or two is no longer shown on its own. The line stays up without blinking while the same sentence continues.

### Also new
- A second Custom LLM provider (Custom LLM 2) for another local or OpenAI-compatible server.
- The generic player finds captions that a page builds itself (as on some learning platforms).
- Retranslating shows a quick translation of the part being played while the LLM translates again.
- Rate-limit waits (HTTP 429) are shared across all requests, and AST learns how many requests a provider allows at once.
- Player menu rows are more compact, the Vimeo menu keeps its inner spacing, and the AST button returns right away when a site is turned back on.
